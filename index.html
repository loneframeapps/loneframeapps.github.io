Wavebead

Wavebead is a record you keep yourself. You can log medications and injections, set reminders, and add weight and check-ins. Estimated levels are calculations from those entries. They are not measurements.

  • Log

    Medications and injections you enter.

  • Reminders

    Reminders for the records you keep.

  • Weight and check-ins

    Weight and short check-ins, kept with the log.

  • Estimated levels

    Calculations from your entries, not readings from a device.

  • Exports and backups

    Files you choose to export, and an optional backup to your own Google Drive.

  • Pro upgrade

    An optional one-time upgrade through Google Play, when the app is available.

Wavebead does not give medical advice and is not a medical device.
