Wavebead

Wavebead is a record you keep yourself. You can log GLP-1 shots and pills, set reminders, and add weight and check-ins. Estimated levels are calculations from those entries. They are not measurements.

  • Log

    Medications, injections, and pills you enter.

  • Reminders

    Reminders for the records you keep.

  • Weight and check-ins

    Weight and short check-ins, kept with the log.

  • Estimated levels

    Calculations from your entries, not readings from a device.

  • Exports and backups

    A file you can share to an app you choose, such as Files or Google Drive, plus up to four weekly backups kept on the phone.

  • Pro upgrade

    An optional one-time upgrade through Google Play.

Wavebead does not give medical advice. Estimated levels are calculations from what you enter, not measurements. Always follow your prescriber and the label.
