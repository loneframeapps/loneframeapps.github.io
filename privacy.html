Wavebead

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Android package com.loneframeapps.wavebead · version code 19 · version name 1.0.0

This policy explains how Wavebead and the Lone Frame Apps website handle your information.

The app

Wavebead is a personal log for medications and injections. You can record doses, set reminders, and add weight, check-ins, and related notes. Estimated levels are calculations from a half-life and the doses you enter. They are not measurements, and they are not medical advice.

Wavebead is from Lone Frame Apps, operated by woodpark picture books (우드파크 픽쳐북스).

What stays on the phone

The log and settings are stored in the app on your device. They can include medication names, doses, dates, injection sites, notes, tags, weight, check-ins, workout notes, reminder settings, and preferences such as a PIN.

A home-screen widget can show medication names, dose, and timing from a copy the app keeps for the widget. Reminders can show on the phone’s notifications. Anyone who can see the screen or the notification shade can see that text.

There is no Wavebead account. The reviewed app does not send the log to a Lone Frame Apps server.

Weekly copies on the phone

The app can save a backup on the phone about once a week, and you can turn that off or save one yourself. It keeps up to four of those files in the app’s private storage. They include the saved log and settings. They are not uploaded by that feature. Turning the weekly backup off stops new ones. It does not delete copies already saved.

Files you export or share

When you ask, the app can make:

  • a CSV of shots, weight, check-ins, and workouts, including medication, dose, site, tags, and notes
  • a JSON backup of the app’s saved state
  • a PDF summary, or an image or video, when you use those share or save actions

On Android, a full backup is written as a file and handed to the system share sheet. You choose the app that receives it, such as Files, Google Drive, or another app. Lone Frame Apps does not connect to Google Drive and does not operate that copy. The app you pick handles the file under its own terms. Google’s policy is at Google Privacy Policy.

Saving to the gallery or downloads places a file in a Wavebead folder in Pictures, Movies, or Downloads. That file stays there until you delete it.

Android’s own backup

This release allows Android backup, and it does not include rules that leave the log out of that backup. If backup is on for the device, Android or Google may copy app data separately from the export and weekly-backup controls above. Clearing the app or uninstalling it does not delete a backup already stored that way. A later install can restore it. Turn device backup off, or delete the existing device backup, if you do not want that copy kept. This behavior was not tried on a phone.

Google Play

Pro is an optional one-time purchase, product wavebead_pro, handled by Google Play. The app asks Play whether that product is owned on the Google account used by the device, and it can restore Pro from that answer. The medication log is not the purchase.

Google processes the payment. Play can also make purchase records available to the developer, such as the fact that this product was bought. This policy does not list every field Play Console can show, and it does not say the developer receives none of them. Card details stay with Google.

The app can open Play’s review prompt and can check Play for an update. Those features use Google Play. They are separate from the log. The packaged app does not declare the Android internet permission. Play’s own apps can still use a network when you buy, restore, review, or update. Checking whether a network is connected is not the same as sending your log.

What the developer receives

Lone Frame Apps does not run a Wavebead login and does not receive the contents of the log through the app. The reviewed direct components do not include an advertising or analytics product. The release does include the Play Billing library. That library brings Google transport components with it. This review did not capture network traffic, so it does not promise that no component can ever contact Google through Play.

If you email loneframeapps@gmail.com, that message goes to that mailbox, including whatever you choose to write. We use support emails to respond to your requests. We delete them within 12 months after your request is resolved, unless longer retention is necessary for legal obligations or disputes. You can request earlier deletion by contacting us.

Permissions and the app lock

The release asks Android for notifications, vibration, biometric or fingerprint unlock, notice after the phone restarts, and a wake lock used with reminders. Saving a file on Android 9 and earlier can ask for storage access. The app does not request camera, location, or contacts.

You can set a PIN. An optional biometric check is done by the phone. The app asks for a yes or no. The PIN and biometric check lock the app. They do not encrypt files you have already exported, and they do not control Android backup.

Deletion

You can delete individual entries in the app. The erase control removes the current log. It does not remove every private copy. Weekly backups, copies kept before an update, import, or restore, and the widget’s saved values can remain.

To clear the app’s private copies, open Android Settings, then Apps, Wavebead, and Storage, and clear the app’s storage. Remove the Wavebead home-screen widget. Delete CSV, JSON, PDF, image, and video files you already saved, including copies in Google Drive or other apps. If device backup is on, also turn it off or delete the existing device backup so Android does not restore the log later. Clearing the app does not delete Google Play purchase records.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages have no account, form, analytics, or advertising, and they do not set their own cookies. GitHub may process connection information, such as an IP address, as the host. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
