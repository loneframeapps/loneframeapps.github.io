Wavebead

Privacy draft

Draft for review · November 8, 2026 · no effective date

Android package com.loneframeapps.wavebead

This is not a finalized privacy policy and it is not a claim of verified compliance. The app was not re-checked for this page. Where behavior is described, that is the intended design and it is still open to correction.

The app

Wavebead is an unpublished Android app from Lone Frame Apps. It is a personal log for medications and injections. The intended app includes reminders, weight and check-ins, estimated levels, exports and backups, and an optional one-time Pro upgrade.

Estimated levels are calculations from what you enter. They are not measurements. Wavebead is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Information you enter

The log can include medications, injections, notes, weight, check-ins, and settings. The intended design keeps that log on your device until you export it or turn on an optional backup. This draft does not promise what is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Collected by the developer

The intended design is that Lone Frame Apps does not offer a Wavebead account and does not receive the contents of your log. That is not an audit. This draft does not claim that the app never uses a network connection.

Google services, and files you choose to export, are separate from information the developer collects. They are described below.

Exports you choose

The intended design creates an export only when you ask for one, and you choose where that file goes. Formats are not listed here until they are checked.

Optional Google Drive backup

An optional backup to your own Google Drive is part of the intended design. If you use it, the file is stored in your Google account. Google’s terms and Google Privacy Policy cover that service. Lone Frame Apps does not operate Google Drive.

This draft does not say whether the developer can view a backup file, and it does not describe security controls.

Google Play purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is intended as a one-time purchase through Google Play, once the app is listed. There is no store listing yet. Google processes the payment. This draft does not list which purchase records Google may share with the developer, and it does not claim that a purchase involves no data at all.

This website

These pages are a static draft. They have no account, form, analytics, or advertising, and they do not set their own cookies. This draft is not hosted. If the site is hosted later, the host may keep its own connection logs.

Permissions, security, and deletion

This draft does not list device permissions, security measures, or deletion steps. Those facts need a check against the app before they can be written as policy.

An earlier draft said the app does not use the internet, does not transfer data, and uses only vibration. This page does not repeat those claims. Optional Google Drive backup and Google Play purchases involve Google services, and the earlier wording was not re-checked.

Children

Wavebead is not directed at children. This draft makes no verified statement about age checks or about information from children.

Changes

A reviewed policy would replace this page and would show an effective date. No effective date is set. An earlier draft date of October 29, 2026 is not used.
