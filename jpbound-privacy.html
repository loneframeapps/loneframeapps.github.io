JP Bound Clinic Finder

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Android package com.loneframeapps.jpbound · version code 19 · version name 0.4.0

This policy explains how JP Bound Clinic Finder handles information. JP Bound Clinic Finder is an information-only guide to clinics in Japan whose own websites say they offer care to visitors. It is from Lone Frame Apps, operated by woodpark picture books (우드파크 픽처북스).

No account, no developer data collection

JP Bound Clinic Finder does not ask you to create an account. Lone Frame Apps does not sell personal data and does not run its own analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad on the clinic list, map, Japan map, favorites, and Info screens. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, and may share them with its advertising partners as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent message first, and you can change that choice later from Settings → Ad privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Lone Frame Apps does not receive these identifiers. Ads for prescription drugs and weight-loss products are blocked in the AdMob account.

Lone Frame Apps does not receive a copy of your favorites, settings, or anything you look at in the app.

What stays on the device

These preferences are stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage, and are not sent to Lone Frame Apps:

  • favorite clinics you mark with the heart
  • language, dark mode, text size, and whether sounds and haptics are on
  • your city choice, trip dates, trip plan, checklist ticks, clinics picked for comparison, and reminder times
  • a copy of the clinic list for offline use
  • whether you have purchased “Remove ads” on this device

Clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling the app, removes those on-device preferences. They are not backed up to a Lone Frame Apps server.

Location (“Near me”)

If you tap Near me or Use my location, Android asks for location permission. Your location is used only on the device to sort clinics by distance. It is not stored and not sent to Lone Frame Apps. You can say no and still use every other feature.

Reminders

If you turn on trip checklist reminders, the app schedules local notifications on the device. Android may ask for notification permission. Nothing is sent to a server.

Directions and the clipboard

When you choose Directions, the app can copy the clinic’s published Japanese address to the device clipboard and then open Google Maps with that clinic as the destination. The address is not sent to Lone Frame Apps. Whatever you copy stays on the device unless you paste it somewhere else.

External links

The app can open:

  • a clinic’s own website
  • Google Maps, including directions, a place listing, hotels nearby, and Street View
  • photos or access pages on a clinic’s site, when the clinic publishes them
  • this privacy policy, when you open it from the app

Those sites and apps are outside JP Bound Clinic Finder. Google Maps, clinic websites, and any other destination have their own terms and privacy policies. JP Bound Clinic Finder does not include hotel-booking affiliate links and does not place ads on those destinations.

The map and clinic list

Clinic facts in the app are compiled from each clinic’s publicly available web pages. The in-app map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Loading the list, the map, and any link you open uses the device’s network connection. The Android package declares the internet permission for that purpose, vibration for optional haptic feedback, location for Near me, notifications for optional reminders, and the advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob.

If you use the share action, Android’s share sheet lets you choose an app to receive a short title, a line of text, and a map or website link. Lone Frame Apps does not operate the app you pick.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages have no account, form, analytics, or advertising, and they do not set their own cookies. GitHub may process connection information, such as an IP address, as the host. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What the developer receives

If you email loneframeapps@gmail.com, that message goes to that mailbox, including whatever you choose to write. We use support emails to respond to your requests. We delete them within 12 months after your request is resolved, unless longer retention is necessary for legal obligations or disputes. You can request earlier deletion by contacting us.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time in-app purchase, “Remove ads,” that hides banners on this device. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, not by Lone Frame Apps. Google handles the payment, and Google may collect purchase information as described in the Google Payments privacy notice and the Google Privacy Policy. Lone Frame Apps can see ordinary Play purchase and order records in Play Console (for example, that a Remove ads purchase was made). We do not receive your full payment card number. The app stores only an on-device flag that ads have been removed, so banners stay hidden. You can use Restore purchase on a new phone to recover a purchase already made on your Google account.

Google Play may also make ordinary store records available to the developer, such as install or crash figures for the app listing. This policy does not list every field Play Console can show.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
