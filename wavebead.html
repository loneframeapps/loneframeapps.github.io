Android app

Wavebead

A gentle GLP-1 shot tracker. Log shots and pills, set reminders, and add weight and check-ins. Estimated levels are calculations from what you enter. They are not measurements.

Privacy policy

What it does

A private log you keep on your phone

Wavebead is a record you keep yourself, for Android. There is no Wavebead account. The app does not send your log to a Lone Frame Apps server.

In the app

Clear records, without the noise

  • Log

    Medications, injections, and pills you enter, including presets for common GLP-1 medicines or your own schedule.

  • Reminders

    Reminders for the records you keep.

  • Weight and check-ins

    Weight and short check-ins, kept with the log.

  • Estimated levels

    Calculations from your entries, not readings from a device.

  • Exports and backups

    A file you can share to an app you choose, such as Files or Google Drive, plus up to four weekly backups kept on the phone.

  • Pro upgrade

    An optional one-time upgrade through Google Play.

Wavebead does not give medical advice, diagnose, or suggest doses or schedules. Estimated levels are calculations from what you enter, not measurements. Always follow your prescriber and the label.
